Intro: We have covariant derivative. Parallel transport solves D V/dt=0 along curve. Given initial vector V(t0) in T_gamma(t0)M, find unique vector field V(t) along gamma satisfying ∇_{dot gamma} V=0. This is ODE. It extends local comparison to finite distance.

1. Уравнение переноса
Let gamma: [a,b]->M, V(t) along curve. Condition:
DV/dt = dV/dt - <dV/dt,n> n =0 (for embedded) or abstract ∇_{dot gamma} V=0.
In coordinates: gamma(t) has coordinates x^i(t), V(t)=V^i(t) e_i (maybe e_i coordinate basis). Then
∇_{dot gamma} V = (dV^k/dt + Γ^k_{ij} dot x^i V^j) e_k =0.
So ODE:
dV^k/dt = - Γ^k_{ij}(gamma(t)) dot x^i(t) V^j(t).
Initial condition V^k(t0)=V_0^k. Linear ODE, unique solution.

2. Why lengths/angles preserved
If V,W parallel transported along same curve, derivative of g(V,W) = g(∇_{dot gamma}V,W)+g(V,∇_{dot gamma}W)=0. Thus inner products constant. In particular length constant.
